## 2. B2B Strategy Revision & Habits Media Network (Link 2)

### Title
*"Strategic Transformation Report: Recalibration Toward Integrated B2B Services and Digital Asset Aggregation (Revision 2.0)"*

### Strategic Pivot - Two Main Initiatives

#### Project 4.1: International Placement & B2B Services
- Shift from episodic events (Summit) to sustainable B2B services
- Stable revenue stream through placement services
- Client onboarding protocols with data-driven media planning

#### Project 4.2: Habits Media Network
- Federated governance model
- "Center of Excellence" for shared services (Ad Ops, Tech, Legal, Finance)
- "Brand Nodes" for creative autonomy

### Operational Details
- B2B client onboarding protocols
- Three-way matching financial reconciliation
- AI-powered media planning

### Implementation Roadmap
- **60-Day Implementation Roadmap**: Granular daily/weekly plan for PMO
- **2-Hour Annual Planning Agenda**: Based on 4DX methodology
- Focus on "Wildly Important Goals" (WIGs) and eliminating "zombie projects"

## 3. Operational Optimization & Six Sigma (Link 3)

### Title
*"Media Agency Operational Transformation: Integration of PMI Portfolio Management Standards, Six Sigma Methodology, and Lean Principles for Strategic Excellence"*

### Key Methodologies

#### Six Sigma (DMAIC)
- Target: Reduce invoice errors from 5% to <1%
- Root cause analysis and automation
- Insertion Order (IO) automation

#### Lean Management
- Applied to Content Supply Chain
- Identify and eliminate "8 Wastes" in creative production
- Value Stream Mapping implementation

#### Kanban
- Visual workflow management for creative teams
- Work In Progress (WIP) limits

### Technology Integration
- Centralized work management platform
- Digital Asset Management (DAM) integration
- Generative AI for repetitive tasks (asset resizing)

### Implementation Phases (12 months)
1. **Phase 1**: Stabilization
2. **Phase 2**: Process Reengineering
3. **Phase 3**: Automation
